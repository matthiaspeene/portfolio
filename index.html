Focused technical-audio engagements for game teams, composers, and audio developers who need to unblock, prove, or finish a specific part of the audio stack.

01

Implementation & performance audit

Find the technical and creative bottlenecks in an existing Unity audio implementation.

Scope
One Unity project using Unity Audio, FMOD, or Wwise. I review architecture, event setup, routing, runtime behaviour, profiling data, and one agreed target platform.
Deliverables
  • Technical review of the project and a reproducible build
  • Prioritised findings with evidence, severity, and recommended fixes
  • Action plan and one follow-up handoff call
Schedule
3–5 working days after access and a reproducible build are available.
Revisions
One correction and clarification round within 5 working days of delivery.
Payment
Fixed fee: 50% to book, 50% when the report is delivered. Invoices are due within 7 days.
02

Vertical-slice audio sprint

Take one playable slice from silent or partial to integrated, reviewable audio.

Scope
One agreed level, encounter, or gameplay loop in Unity with FMOD or Wwise, covering core SFX, ambience, music states, mixer routing, and gameplay hooks. Project access and approved source assets are supplied unless asset creation is included in the proposal.
Deliverables
  • An integrated project branch or implementation package
  • A representative slice with an initial mix pass
  • Handoff notes and a walkthrough for the team
Schedule
1–2 weeks from kickoff, depending on project and asset readiness.
Revisions
One consolidated revision pass against the agreed acceptance criteria.
Payment
Fixed fee: 50% at kickoff, 50% after review and before final handoff. Invoices are due within 7 days.
03

Adaptive-music or rhythm prototype

Prove the feel and technical shape of an interactive music idea before full production.

View scope & terms Hide scope & terms +
Scope
One core musical interaction, such as layered or stem-based transitions, intensity control, beat-synchronised events, or a rhythm input and scoring loop, built with supplied or placeholder music.
Deliverables
  • A playable prototype with source
  • Tunable parameters and a lightweight debug view
  • A recorded demo, technical notes, and next-step recommendations
Schedule
1–2 weeks from an approved interaction brief and usable music assets.
Revisions
One consolidated tuning pass on the agreed musical interaction.
Payment
Fixed fee: 50% at kickoff, 50% after prototype review and before source handoff. Invoices are due within 7 days.
04

Custom audio tool or plugin prototype

Turn one repetitive workflow or technical question into something the team can test.

View scope & terms Hide scope & terms +
Scope
One defined workflow in one target environment—for example, a Unity editor tool, Reaper script, JUCE plugin, or standalone utility. Prototype-grade UX is included; production hardening, signing, installers, and multi-platform support are separate scopes.
Deliverables
  • A working prototype with source
  • An example project or focused test case
  • Build and setup notes plus a recorded walkthrough
Schedule
2–4 weeks from approval of the functional specification.
Revisions
One consolidated functional revision pass; new features are scoped separately.
Payment
Milestone fee: 40% to book, 30% at working-prototype review, and 30% before source and build handoff. Invoices are due within 7 days.

These are starting scopes, not off-the-shelf packages. Every engagement begins with a short fit call and a written fixed-fee proposal. Final scope, schedule, and fee are agreed before work begins; additions are quoted separately.
