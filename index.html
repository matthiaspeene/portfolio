Why NotJustSound?

Sound does more than you might think. It can tell you where an item is, how strong an attack is, or whether something needs your attention. It also shapes how players feel. The right music at the right time can make anyone cry. I don’t just make sounds, I make sound systems. I don’t just implement those systems, I use them to provide feedback and shape the player’s emotions when it counts. Sound is a massive invisible force behind game feel. That’s why I call my business NotJustSound.
